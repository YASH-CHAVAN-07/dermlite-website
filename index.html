DermLite DL5 Plus

Clinical Release Available for Direct Practice & Hospital Orders

The flagship DermLite DL5 Plus is the pinnacle of optical dermoscopy. Features continuous variable polarization, 365 nm UV mode twice as bright as DL5, 9-level PigmentBoost®, true 10x magnification, and native DermLite App integration.

5-Year DermLite Warranty

Comprehensive Engineering

Features & Benefits

Engineered from surgical grade alloys and world-class optics for daily professional excellence.

OPTICS

  • ø32 mm premium four-element lens system with exceptional clarity up-close and at distance
  • True 10x magnification — resolves even finer micro-structures
  • Works with virtually any smartphone via included MCC universal adapter

POLARIZATION

  • Toggle between DermLite-quality polarized and non-polarized illumination
  • Scroll continuously from deep cross-polarization to extremely superficial parallel polarization
  • Enhanced brightness across all modes for improved vascular visualization

ILLUMINATION

  • Substantially brighter white LEDs with signature DermLite spectrum
  • 365 nm UV mode — more than twice as bright as DL5
  • PigmentBoost® mode with 9 adjustable color temperature levels
  • Torch mode: ultrabright LED for distant illumination
  • 9 levels of brightness control in all modes

SMART CONNECTIVITY

  • Control lighting modes from your phone via the dedicated DermLite App
  • Automated MultiShot (multiple lighting modes captured with a single tap)
  • AI risk scoring powered by FotoFinder (MDR-compliant where available)

DESIGN

  • Removable glass contact plate with 10 mm reticle (bayonet style)
  • Includes IceCap disposable caps to help reduce cross-contamination
  • Includes magnetically attached 100 mm stainless steel ruler
  • Works with virtually any smartphone via included MCC adapter
  • Compatible with small-area contact plate (sold separately)

POWER

  • 3000 mAh lithium-ion battery
  • Four-level charge indicator; device sleeps after 3 minutes of inactivity
  • USB-C charging with included 2m USB-C cable
  • Vacation Mode reduces battery drain during extended downtime
