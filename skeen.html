MultiShot™

One capture. Multiple views.

A single tap captures polarized, non-polarized and UV images of the same spot — three perspectives on every lesion, with no extra steps.

Non-polarized

Shows surface morphology and superficial detail.

Cross-polarized

Cuts reflections to reveal deeper pigmented and vascular structures.

UV light

Makes fluorescence patterns visible that ordinary light cannot show.

Technical details

Features & Benefits

OPTICS

  • Two-element lens system, 15 mm diameter
  • True 10× magnification for detailed examination
  • 25 mm effective focal length
  • Anti-reflective lens coating

ILLUMINATION

  • 4 cross-polarized and 4 parallel-polarized white LEDs
  • 4 × 365 nm and 4 × 405 nm UV LEDs
  • White light colour temperature 5,000–8,000 K
  • High brightness of ≥ 10 kLux
  • UV intensity of ≥ 10 mW/cm²

LIGHTING MODES

  • Cross-polarized light
  • Non-polarized light
  • Parallel-polarized light
  • 365 nm UV light
  • 405 nm UV light

CONTROLS & FUNCTIONS

  • Automated MultiShot — several lighting modes captured with a single tap
  • AI risk scoring (MDR-compliant where available)
  • Trichoscopy analysis powered by TrichoLAB

POWER

  • 5,000 mAh lithium-polymer battery
  • Up to 6 hours of continuous use at maximum brightness
  • Over 75 % charge in about 1.5 hours
  • USB-C charging or charging base

DESIGN

  • Handheld imaging unit with 6.7″ touchscreen
  • Removable dermoscopy lens
  • IceCap™ disposable covers for hygiene
  • One-piece body — no smartphone or adapter needed
